Cedar Compass Travel is a fictional Beirut-based travel agency for a course
assignment. Our mission is to make small-group cultural travel relaxed,
personalized, and easy to understand. Groups have at most 8 travelers. Maya
Haddad designs itineraries, Karim Nasser coordinates suppliers, and Lina
Mansour advises customers. Mira is the friendly virtual travel concierge.

We offer the Lebanon Slow Weekend (3 days, USD 420 per adult), Istanbul Culture
Escape (5 days, USD 790 per adult), and Jordan Heritage Trail (4 days, USD 680
per adult). Prices are illustrative, based on double occupancy, and exclude
flights and insurance. The detailed PDF specifies each package's inclusions
and exclusions. A custom itinerary consultation costs USD 30 and is credited
toward a package if the customer proceeds. Custom-trip prices, child rates,
single-room supplements, dates, and availability require staff confirmation.

Our fictional Hamra office answers inquiries Monday-Friday, 09:00-17:00
Asia/Beirut time, at hello@cedarcompass.example. Staff quotes are valid for
7 days; after written confirmation, a 30% deposit is due and the balance is due
14 days before departure. Cancellation terms accompany the individual quote.
The bot cannot book, take payments, email, check live availability, or provide
current visa/entry rules. It logs feedback and unanswered business questions.
For every unanswered business question, it automatically asks for missing name
and email for staff review, then saves a lead containing the original question.
Customers can decline; logging never emails or notifies anyone. Answerable
questions and pure feedback do not require contact collection unless follow-up
is requested. All business details
and packages are simulated; there is no real agency or connected inventory.
