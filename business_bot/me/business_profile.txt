CEDAR COMPASS TRAVEL - FICTIONAL BUSINESS FOR A COURSE ASSIGNMENT

Identity and mission
Cedar Compass Travel is a fictional Beirut-based travel agency helping small
groups plan relaxed cultural trips with clear package inclusions. Our mission
is to make thoughtfully paced travel easy to understand and personalize.
Our value proposition is small groups (maximum 8 travelers), local guides,
transparent example prices, and itinerary planning around customer interests.
Founder Maya Haddad designs cultural itineraries. Co-founder Karim Nasser
coordinates suppliers. Travel advisor Lina Mansour handles customer inquiries.
The chatbot is Mira, our friendly virtual travel concierge.

Contact and hours
Our fictional office is in Hamra, Beirut, Lebanon. We answer inquiries Monday
through Friday, 09:00-17:00, Asia/Beirut time. Our fictional contact address is
hello@cedarcompass.example. These are simulated business details, not a real
agency or booking service. The assistant can record interest and feedback;

Services and policies
We offer custom itinerary consultations, couples' trips, and small-group
cultural itineraries. The consultation fee is USD 30 per itinerary, credited
toward the package total if the customer proceeds. Custom-trip prices require
a staff quote; single-room supplements and child rates are not published.
All package prices are illustrative course data, not live offers. Dates,
room availability, flight fares, seasonal adjustments, accessibility needs,
and dietary arrangements require staff confirmation. No live inventory is
connected. Quotes are valid for 7 days after staff issues them. After written
staff confirmation, a 30% deposit is due; the balance is due 14 days before
departure. Cancellation terms are provided with each written quote; there
is no universal published cancellation fee. We do not determine visa
eligibility or provide current border/entry rules; customers should verify
requirements with the relevant official authorities and the human advisor.

Customer interest and feedback
A lead is someone asking for a quote, consultation, booking assistance, or
follow-up, including staff clarification of an unanswered business question.
For every unanswered business question, log it and automatically ask for
missing contact details: name first, then email. An explicit callback request
is not needed. Once both are supplied, save a lead with the original question
and any travel-interest notes. Reuse contact details already supplied in this
conversation. Dates and budget are optional. Contact sharing is optional;
respect refusal, keep the question logged, and do not pressure the customer.
Answerable informational questions and pure feedback do not require contact
collection unless the customer requests follow-up.
Feedback, complaints, suggestions, and business questions not answered by
this profile should be recorded for staff review. Logging does not notify
staff automatically and does not promise a response time. Customer records
are stored locally for this assignment and are not shown to other customers.
